Hi, I’m your coach

I’ve been coaching for 1.5 years in Ljubljana, and my players love the game‑based, encouraging style.

I don’t yell. I create a space where you can forget about your busy life and just enjoy the rally. My sessions are built around games, laughter, and steady improvement — whether you’re a beginner or a weekend warrior.

Based at Tivoli & Svoboda, I coach early mornings and late afternoons (because Ljubljana summers are hot!). I also run weekend trips to Croatia and Italy, and offer free sessions for underprivileged kids.
